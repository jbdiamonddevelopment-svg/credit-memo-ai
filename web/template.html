    How this works

    • Annual figures come from each company's 10-K XBRL data on SEC EDGAR. A Python engine maps US-GAAP tags to a bank-style spread (revenue, EBITDA, debt, cash flow).
    • EBITDA is operating income plus D&A, with non-cash goodwill and intangible impairments added back. Total debt is funded debt only (operating leases excluded).
    • The scorecard weights leverage (25%), interest coverage (20%), FCF/debt (15%), EBITDA margin (15%), liquidity (10%), scale (10%) and EBITDA trend (5%). Negative equity triggers a one-notch downgrade.
    • The facility request, covenants and decision are illustrative, sized at about 0.25x EBITDA, and stress-tested at EBITDA −20% / +200bp and −35% / +300bp.
    • Portfolio project for learning purposes. Not investment advice or an actual lending decision, and not an agency rating.
